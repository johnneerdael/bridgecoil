# Artwork and font usage

The original Bridgecoil artwork and UI studies are supplied for reuse and adaptation in your project. Editable SVGs are the source of truth. Use the same identity proportions and semantic roles across platforms.

The included fonts retain their separate SIL Open Font License terms. Keep the supplied OFL text files with redistributed font binaries. Third-party platform and technology names retain their respective ownership; no affiliation is implied by descriptive references.
