# Get started

<p class="lead">Bridgecoil runs on your phone and manages your Android TV over your home network. These pages take you from installing the app to a TV that's set up the way you want.</p>

Work through them in this order the first time:

1. [Install Bridgecoil on your phone](install.md).
2. [Turn on debugging on your TV](prepare-your-tv.md). This is what lets a phone manage the TV.
3. [Connect your phone to the TV](first-connection.md).
4. [Find your way around the app](find-your-way-around.md).
5. [Set up your TV](set-up-your-tv.md), so that button changes survive a restart.

You can look around the app after step 3 without changing anything. Bridgecoil shows you every change before it runs, and nothing happens until you apply it.

If you already know what you want, go to [Pick a guide for your goal](choose-your-path.md).
