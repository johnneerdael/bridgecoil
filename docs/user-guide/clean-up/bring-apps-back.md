# Bring apps back

<p class="lead">Everything Clean up turned off can be turned back on.</p>

Open **Tools → Clean up**. It says how many apps are off, with **Turn {n} apps back on**. It does up to 32 at a time, and it covers apps rated Recommended.

To bring back one app, use its row in [History](../history/undo-a-change.md), or open **Apps**, filter by **Disabled**, open the app and choose **Enable**.
