# Update a TV's address

<p class="lead">Use this when Quick connect can't find the TV, for example after its port changed.</p>

1. Open Saved TVs and tap **Update connection address** on the TV's row.
2. On the TV, open the main Wireless debugging screen and read its address and port.
3. Type them into **Connection address** and tap **Connect to TV →**.

Bridgecoil still checks that this is the same TV, so you can't overwrite a saved TV with a different one by accident.

Wireless debugging picks a new port when it restarts. Giving your TV a fixed address in your router's settings keeps the first part of the address the same, which makes this rarer.
