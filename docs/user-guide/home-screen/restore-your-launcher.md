# Go back to your TV's own launcher

<p class="lead">Turn the launcher your TV came with back on and make it the default.</p>

If your stock launcher is off, Home app shows **Restore your TV's launcher** at the bottom. Tap it, read the review and apply it. Bridgecoil turns the launcher back on and makes it the default in one step, then checks that Android agrees.

!!! screenshot "home-restore.png"
    Home app with Restore your TV's launcher at the bottom.

If you see **Restore your TV's launcher** with a package name in brackets, your TV has more than one stock launcher that's off. Choose the one you want.

Setup wizards also count as home screens to Android. Bridgecoil never offers those, because turning one on could restart first-time setup.

Your other launcher stays on after this. Stock launchers usually have priority, so the stock one takes over.

## To undo a switch instead

If you switched between launchers, open the switch in [History](../history/undo-a-change.md) and tap **Review recovery**. That turns back on whatever the switch turned off and makes your old launcher the default again.

## On Android 11 and earlier

Bridgecoil can't list disabled launchers exactly on some older TVs, so it finds them by name. It turns the launcher on, and the launcher's priority takes Home back.

## If the button isn't there

The stock launcher is still on, or Bridgecoil couldn't identify it. Open **Apps**, filter by **Disabled**, and enable it there. See [Turn off, stop or remove an app](../apps/turn-off-or-remove-an-app.md).
