# Replace your home screen

<p class="lead">Try a launcher first, then switch to it. Switching makes it the default, opens it and turns the old one off in one step.</p>

!!! screenshot "home-list.png"
    Home app with the four launchers and In use at the top.

1. Open **Tools → Home app**. If the launcher you want has **Install**, [install it](install-a-launcher.md) first.
2. Tap **Try it** next to the launcher. It opens on the TV, and nothing has changed yet.
3. Move around with your remote. Set the launcher up if it asks you to, and give it any permission it asks for.
4. In Bridgecoil, tap **Yes, it works**, or **No** to stay where you are.
5. Read the review and apply it.

!!! screenshot "home-try.png"
    Did it open, with Yes, it works and No.

!!! screenshot "home-switch-review.png"
    The Switch Home app review showing what will be turned off.

## What the switch does

After you apply the review, Bridgecoil runs three commands in order, and stops if one fails:

1. Makes the new launcher the default.
2. Opens it.
3. Turns off the launcher you were using, and any other launcher that's taking over Home.

It only reports **Verified** when Android says the new launcher is the default and the old ones are off. That also covers your TV's stock launcher. The old launcher keeps its data, so you can [undo the switch](../history/undo-a-change.md) from History.

The old launcher has to go because some launchers take the Home button back while they're installed. Projectivy, for example, has an option that does this through an accessibility service. A launcher that's off can't do that, and it can't appear in place of your new one after a restart.

!!! warning "Some TV features can depend on the stock launcher"
    On some TVs, the settings shortcut, the input list or the app drawer belongs to the stock launcher. If something goes missing after a switch, [go back to your TV's launcher](restore-your-launcher.md).

## After you switch

A launcher stays your default after a restart, so it needs no [TV setup](../get-started/set-up-your-tv.md). On Android 9, Bridgecoil keeps your choice on your phone, because the TV has no way to report it.

## Turning the stock launcher off separately

If you switched launchers some other way, **Your launcher is in charge** offers **Turn off {name}** for each stock launcher still on.
