# Use your phone as a remote

<p class="lead">Open **Remote** to send button presses and text to the TV.</p>

!!! screenshot "remote-page.png"
    The Remote page.

You get arrow buttons, **OK**, **Back**, **Home** and **Menu**, playback buttons (**Previous**, **Play / pause**, **Next**) and volume (**Volume −**, **Mute**, **Volume +**).

Tick **Send as long press** to send every press as a long press until you untick it. This reaches menus that need holding a button.

The line at the bottom says **Sent · check the TV** after a press. That means the command ran, not that the TV reacted, so look at the screen. There's no power button.

## Type on the TV

Typing with a TV remote is slow. Select a text field on the TV first, then tap **Type on TV**, enter your text and tap **Send text**. The dialog stays open, so you can correct your text and send it again.

!!! screenshot "remote-type.png"
    The Type on TV dialog.

This sends plain English letters, numbers and symbols, up to 1024 characters. Accented letters and the % sign don't work this way, so use the TV's keyboard for those.

Your text is sent to the TV as a command. On a legacy connection that isn't encrypted, so avoid typing passwords this way.

## Open an app

**Open an app** takes you to [Apps](../apps/open-an-app.md).

**Remap your remote's buttons** opens [Buttons](../buttons/index.md).

Button presses appear in [History](../history/index.md) with names such as `Remote · up`. Nothing on this page goes through a review, because a key press changes nothing that lasts.
