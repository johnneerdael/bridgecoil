# Open an app or a specific screen

<p class="lead">Start an app on the TV from your phone, including apps that don't appear on the home screen.</p>

Open the app in **Apps** and tap **Open app** on the **Overview** tab. Bridgecoil prefers the TV version of the app when it has both. You'll see "Open request sent. Check the TV."

**No launch activity reported** means the app has no screen to open, which is common for background services.

## Open a specific screen

Some apps have several entry points. If so, the **Details** tab shows **Choose a launch activity**, which lists them. This is useful for reaching a settings screen that an app hides from its menus.

**Details** also shows the app's signing certificate and the full system report for the app, which is what to send someone helping you with a problem.
