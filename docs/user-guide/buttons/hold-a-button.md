# Do something different when you hold a button

<p class="lead">Keep a button's normal action and add a second one when you hold it.</p>

1. Follow [Make a button open the app you want](remap-a-button.md) up to the choice screen.
2. Under **When you**, choose **Hold it down**.
3. Choose what it should do.

A press stays as it is. Holding for over half a second does this instead. The action happens when you let go.

One button can have one press mapping and one hold mapping. If a button only has a press mapping, a long hold also fires it.

Android gives some buttons their own long press action, and that still happens. Test it on your TV.
