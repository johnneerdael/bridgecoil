# Replace what a button opens

<p class="lead">Android can't be told to ignore a button. To replace what a button opens, turn off the app it opens, so that only your mapping is left.</p>

Say your remote has a button for a store app you never use. If you map it to Plex, pressing it opens the store and then Plex. Turning off the store app means the button only opens Plex.

When you've saved a mapping, Bridgecoil knows which app the press opened, and asks:

!!! screenshot "buttons-followup.png"
    This button still opens, with Turn off and Keep it.

**This button still opens {app}.** Choose **Turn off {app}** and apply the review, or **Keep it** to leave both happening.

Turning an app off is the same as [Disable](../apps/turn-off-or-remove-an-app.md). Its data stays on the TV, it stays off after a restart, and you can turn it back on from History or Apps.

If the review shows a warning, read it. The app may be something your TV needs. Check its [rating](../apps/ratings.md) first.

## If you see no offer

"That press didn't open an app, so there's nothing to turn off." means Bridgecoil didn't see an app start. If the button was already on that app, press it from the TV's Home screen while listening, so that the app really opens.

Hold mappings get no offer, because a hold leaves the normal press alone.

## If the button does something other than open an app

A button that opens a TV menu, rather than an app, keeps doing that. You can only turn off an app, and turning off a system menu is usually a bad idea.
