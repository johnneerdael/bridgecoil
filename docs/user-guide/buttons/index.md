# Take back your remote

<p class="lead">Make any button that reaches Android open the app you want, or act as a different button. Open **Tools → Buttons**.</p>

- [Make a button open the app you want](remap-a-button.md)
- [Replace what a button opens](replace-what-a-button-opens.md)
- [Do something different when you hold a button](hold-a-button.md)
- [Test, pause or remove your mappings](test-pause-remove.md)
- [Which buttons can be remapped](which-buttons.md)

For mappings to survive a restart, finish [TV setup](../get-started/set-up-your-tv.md) first. Without it, a mapping works until the TV restarts.

If you're curious how it works, see [How your buttons work](../how-it-works/how-buttons-work.md).
