# Take a screenshot or recording

<p class="lead">Capture what's on the TV, straight to your phone.</p>

Streaming apps usually appear black, because they protect their video. That's Android's protection, and Bridgecoil can't get around it.

Captures go to your phone by themselves, and nothing stays on the TV. On Android 10 and later they land in your phone's **Download** folder with no questions. Android 7 to 9, iPhone and desktop can't do that, so a save dialog opens at the same moment. The file name is `tv-` plus the date and time. Once it's in Download, **Open screenshot** or **Open recording** shows it in your phone's default app.

## Take a screenshot

Open **Tools → Screen capture** and tap **Take a screenshot**. The picture appears in the app and is saved straight away. If you close a save dialog without choosing, **Save screenshot** lets you try again.

## Record the screen

1. Under **Record for**, choose **15 seconds**, **30 seconds**, **1 minute** or **3 minutes**.
2. Tap **Start recording**. You can leave this screen, and the recording carries on.
3. It stops at the time you chose, or when you tap **Stop recording**.
4. Bridgecoil copies it to your phone, then deletes it from the TV.

The recording is only deleted from the TV once it's safely on your phone. If the copy fails, or you close a save dialog, it stays on the TV and you get **Save recording** and **Discard**. If you left the app while it was recording, the copy starts when you come back to Screen capture.

Captures have no review and don't appear in History.

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| "This TV didn't provide a screenshot." | The TV refused, or it's showing protected video |
| "This TV doesn't allow its screen to be recorded." | The TV has no working screen recorder |
| "The TV is already recording its screen." | Wait, or stop the running recording |
