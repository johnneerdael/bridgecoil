# Tools

<p class="lead">Open **Tools** to see all eleven at once. Most need no setup at all.</p>

| Tool | What it's for | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **TV setup** | Keeps button mappings running after a restart | [Set up your TV](../get-started/set-up-your-tv.md) |
| **Buttons** | Remap your remote | [Take back your remote](../buttons/index.md) |
| **Clean up** | Turn off apps you don't need | [Clean up your TV](../clean-up/clean-up-your-tv.md) |
| **Commands** | Ready-made actions, with no typing | [Run a command without typing](commands.md) |
| **Home app** | Change your home screen | [Replace your home screen](../home-screen/index.md) |
| **Screen capture** | Screenshots and recordings | [Take a screenshot or recording](screen-capture.md) |
| **Files** | Browse the TV's storage | [Move files to and from your TV](files.md) |
| **Diagnostics** | Logs and system reports | [Find out why something isn't working](diagnostics.md) |
| **Settings** | Five Android settings | [Change hidden settings](settings.md) |
| **Network** | The TV's addresses and routes | [See your TV's network details](network.md) |
| **Shell** | Run a command you type | [Run your own command](shell.md) |

!!! screenshot "tools-overview.png"

Inside a tool, **All tools** takes you back, and the refresh icon reads the TV again.
